# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — CryptoGPT

**Product Name:** CryptoGPT  
**Category:** AI Cryptocurrency Assistant & Market Intelligence SaaS  
**Target Benchmark:** ChatGPT + Linear + Notion + Apple HIG  
**Theme:** Premium Light Theme Only (`#F7F8FA` Canvas, `#FFFFFF` Cards, `#5B5CEB` Primary, `#ECECEC` Borders)

---

## 1. Executive Overview
CryptoGPT provides an ultra-fast, aesthetically flawless AI assistant specialized in blockchain protocols, tokenomics, DeFi liquidity, technical analysis, and real-time market data.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Dimensions
- **Sidebar**: 280px fixed width on desktop, collapsible drawer on mobile/tablet.
- **Center Chat**: Max-width 820px, 8-point vertical rhythm, auto-scrolling with unread badge.
- **Right Insights**: 340px width, hosting Market Overview, Fear & Greed Gauge, Top Coins, Interactive Price Charts, and Latest News.
- **Color Tokens**:
  - Background: `#F7F8FA`
  - Cards: `#FFFFFF`
  - Primary: `#5B5CEB`
  - Primary Gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #5B5CEB 0%, #7677F4 100%)`
  - Border: `#ECECEC`
  - Text Primary: `#111111`
  - Text Secondary: `#666666`
  - Radius: `20px`

---

## 3. Comprehensive Feature Set
1. **Interactive Conversations**: Streaming simulated responses, step-by-step thinking breakdown ("Deep Research"), code syntax highlighting, tables, and formula rendering.
2. **Action Capabilities**: Thumbs up/down with feedback, Copy response, Text-to-Speech audio reader with animated sound equalizer, Stop & Regenerate.
3. **Multimodal Input**: Voice recording with live audio visualizer, file/image upload with drag & drop.
4. **Market Intelligence**: Live tickers, animated SVG charts, Fear & Greed index, Portfolio P&L tracker, Watchlist, Target price alerts.
5. **Command Palette (`⌘K`)**: Instant fuzzy search across chats, tokens, tools, and actions.
